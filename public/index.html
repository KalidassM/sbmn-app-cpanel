About Us

By Residents, for Our Community

Sri Balamurugan Nagar Welfare Association brings residents together to care for our neighbourhood and improve everyday life in the Nagar.

We work closely with residents and local authorities on matters such as street lighting, roads, drainage, waste management, water supply, safety, and other shared concerns.

Beyond civic needs, the association helps build a stronger community through neighbourhood events, welfare initiatives, regular meetings, and open communication. Every household is encouraged to participate, share concerns, contribute ideas, and be part of shaping a better Nagar.

Civic Upkeep

Working with local authorities on streetlights, drainage, roads, waste management, and other neighbourhood infrastructure.

Community Welfare

Supporting resident well-being, community celebrations, neighbourhood initiatives, and activities that bring people together.

Open & Transparent

Keeping residents informed through notices, meeting updates, decisions, and important association information.
